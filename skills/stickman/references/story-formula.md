# Story formula (from the most-viewed stick-figure videos)
Top performers are Alan Becker's: Animation vs. Minecraft shorts (Cave Spider Roller Coaster ~420-520M views reported), Animation vs. Minecraft original (~320M), Animator vs. Animation IV. What they share:
1. Instant hook: a stick figure + a strange object/power in frame 1 (a glowing cube, a monitor, a cave). No slow intro.
2. A clear want and a clear enemy/obstacle (spiders, the animator, a rival with the same power).
3. ESCALATION in rising beats: each 3-5 s the stakes, speed or absurdity go up (chase -> trap -> bigger chase -> twist). Never a calm middle.
4. Physical comedy and spectacle: big motion, impacts, chain reactions, one visual gag per beat. Characters are colour-coded (Red, Yellow, Blue, Green...) so the audience tracks them without dialogue.
5. A satisfying turn/payoff (they outsmart the enemy, the power backfires, reversal) and a short sting/laugh at the end. No moral lecture.
6. Mostly no dialogue: story told by action; music + SFX carry mood.
Use for /stickman: offer stories with this shape (action-comedy, chase, power-gone-wrong, rivalry, survival, heist, game-world). Gentle slice-of-life only if the user asks for cozy and still needs a hook + a twist. Each story pitch must state: hook (frame 1), enemy/obstacle, 3 escalation beats, twist/payoff.
